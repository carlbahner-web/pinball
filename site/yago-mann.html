Yago Mann

Producer, Mixer, Guitarist

Yago has spent his career helping artists sound more like themselves. First as a guitar player on other people’s records, and now as a producer, his work is built around drawing out the version of the song you’ve been chasing – not imposing his own sound on top of it.

He hears performance before he hears production, which means the take you love but keep second-guessing is often the one he’ll fight for.

The sound he gravitates toward is what he calls “melancholy with momentum”: sweet vocals over distorted textures, drums that hit before you’ve decided to move, bittersweet without becoming heavy.

You won’t leave a session sounding more like Yago. You’ll leave sounding more like yourself – with someone who never made it about him.

Get to know Yago
in four questions:

1. What’s a record that changed how you hear music?
Def Leppard’s Hysteria, The Beatles’ Abbey Road, Pink Floyd’s Dark Side of the Moon.
2. What’s a trait you value in creative people?
Those who are willing to try an idea out even if it sounds ridiculous to them.
3. What’s something you think people take too seriously?
The never ending battle between analog vs digital, plugins vs gear, real amps vs amp sims. Who cares…
4. Coffee or Tea?
Double irish breakfast black tea with a splash of milk and agave.
